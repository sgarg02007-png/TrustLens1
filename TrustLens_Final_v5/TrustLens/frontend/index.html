How TrustLens works

1. Add the news

Paste text, drop a link, or upload a photo, PDF or video caption.

2. We analyze it

Tone, sourcing and evidence are checked by rules and, optionally, AI.

3. You get a rating

A clear verdict out of 100 with reasons you can verify yourself.

What you get

Core claim

The main point of the story in plain language.

What is right

Credible, well-sourced parts of the content.

What is wrong

Errors, unsupported claims and manipulation signs.

Trust rating

Stars, a 0 to 100 score and a graphical summary.

TrustLens gives credibility estimates, not proof. Confirm important claims with trusted sources.
